Why Grading Scale Matters

Choosing the Right Grading Scale

Not every university calculates CGPA the same way. A grade of "A" might equal 4.00 at one institution and 3.75 at another, and some countries use entirely different scales: 5.0, 7.0, or 10.0: instead of the common 4.0 system. Using a calculator built for the wrong scale will give you an inaccurate CGPA, which matters when you're applying for scholarships, jobs, or graduate programs.

That's why this site offers a dedicated calculator for each major grading system, listed above. Pick the one that matches your official transcript, and you'll get an accurate, credit-weighted CGPA with multi-semester tracking, SGPA conversion, a target CGPA planner, and a downloadable PDF report: all for free.
