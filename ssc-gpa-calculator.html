Overview

What Is an SSC GPA Calculator?

This SSC GPA calculator works out your Secondary School Certificate GPA in Bangladesh from either subject grades or subject marks. It turns each subject into a letter grade and grade point, adds the fourth subject bonus, checks for a failed subject and shows the final GPA on the 5.00 scale with every step of the working.

Use it as an SSC result calculator to check the GPA on your marksheet, or as an SSC grade calculator to see which grade a mark gives. The grade scale is the same for the nine general education boards, so there is no board to select.

SSC results are reported as GPA, not as a university-style CGPA. After SSC, use the HSC GPA Calculator for your college results, the CGPA Calculator once you reach university, or the Percentage Converter when a form asks for a percentage.

Step-by-Step

How to Use This SSC GPA Calculator

1

Select Your Group

Choose Science, Business Studies or Humanities. The subject list changes to match your group. Science counts 9 main subjects. Business Studies and Humanities count 10.

2

Choose Grade or Marks

Choose Grade to pick each subject’s letter grade from your marksheet, or Marks to type each subject’s total. Bangla and English are out of 200 (both papers together), ICT is out of 50 and most other subjects are out of 100.

3

Set Your Fourth Subject

Pick your fourth subject and enter its grade or marks. Leave it as None if you do not have one. Only the grade point above 2.00 counts as bonus.

4

Use Grade Mode for Failed Parts

Marks mode cannot see separate paper, MCQ, written or practical pass marks. If your marksheet shows F in any part, switch to Grade and select F for that subject.

5

Press Calculate GPA

Your final GPA, pass or fail status, main subjects GPA and fourth subject bonus appear in the result box. Open Show calculation to see every step.

Understand the Numbers

How SSC GPA Is Calculated

Add the grade points of your main subjects, add the fourth subject bonus, then divide by the number of main subjects. Science has 9 main subjects. Business Studies and Humanities have 10. The final GPA is capped at 5.00.

GPA = (main GP total + fourth subject bonus) ÷ number of main subjects

Bangla and English are one subject each even though each has two papers, so enter the combined total out of 200. The calculator divides your marks by the selected full marks to get a percentage, then applies the fixed grade bands. It never rounds a percentage up to the next grade.

How the Fourth Subject Works

The fourth subject, also called the optional subject, adds only the grade point above 2.00: bonus = max(0, fourth subject GP - 2.00). An A+ adds 3.00, A adds 2.00, A- adds 1.50 and B adds 1.00. C, D and F add zero. The bonus goes into the total, not onto the GPA directly, and the fourth subject is never part of the divisor.

Reaching the Top Grade

How to Get GPA 5 in SSC

GPA 5.00 needs an effective total of at least 5.00 for every main subject. That is 45.00 for Science and 50.00 for Business Studies and Humanities. The fourth subject bonus counts toward that total, so a strong fourth subject lets you lose points in the main subjects. The table shows the lowest main subject total that still gives 5.00.

Lowest main subject grade point total that gives GPA 5.00, by fourth subject grade
Fourth subject gradeBonusScience (9 main subjects)Business Studies and Humanities (10 main subjects)

In Science with A+ in the fourth subject, 6 A+ and 3 A in the main subjects give 42.00. The bonus of 3.00 lifts the total to 45.00, which gives 5.00. In Business Studies or Humanities with the same fourth subject, 7 A+ and 3 A give 47.00 and reach 50.00. A GPA above 5.00 is capped, so extra points add nothing.

Worked Examples

SSC GPA Calculation Examples

Take a Science student with A+ in six of the nine main subjects, A in the other three and A+ in the fourth subject.

Main GP total: 6 × 5 + 3 × 442.00
Fourth subject bonus: 5 - 2+3.00
Effective total45.00
Final GPA: 45 ÷ 95.00

Without the fourth subject the same student gets 42 ÷ 9 = 4.67. The bonus is worth 0.33 of a GPA point here.

A Humanities student with 10 main subjects, a main grade point total of 41.00 and an A- in the fourth subject gets a bonus of 1.50. The GPA is (41.00 + 1.50) ÷ 10 = 4.25. Without the fourth subject it is 41.00 ÷ 10 = 4.10.

Pass, Fail and Golden A+

Failing a Subject and Golden A+

An F in any main subject means the overall result is Fail, even if the other grade points would give a high average. The calculator names the failed subjects and does not show a final GPA.

An F in the fourth subject adds no bonus. It does not fail you overall when all main subjects pass. A high total never cancels a known failure in a paper, MCQ, written or practical part.

Golden A+ is the informal name for A+ in every subject, including the fourth subject. It is not a separate grade and this calculator does not calculate it. GPA 5.00 is a different thing and can be reached with some main subjects below A+, as the table above shows.

Boards and Groups

Education Boards This Calculator Covers

The grade bands apply to the nine general education boards: Dhaka, Rajshahi, Cumilla, Jashore, Chattogram, Barishal, Sylhet, Dinajpur and Mymensingh. Science, Business Studies and Humanities are the three groups built into the calculator.

The Madrasah Education Board (Dakhil) and the Technical Education Board also publish SSC equivalent results, but their subject lists are different. If you sit one of those exams, check which subjects your marksheet counts before you trust the divisor.

Older Results

SSC GPA and the Old Division System

Results before the letter grade system were given as first, second or third division. A government decision reported in 2009 equates SSC and HSC results from 2004 onward with the GPA ranges below. If a form still asks for your division, confirm what the receiving institution accepts.
