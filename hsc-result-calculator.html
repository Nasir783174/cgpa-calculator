Overview

What Is the HSC Result Calculator?

The HSC result calculator works out your Higher Secondary Certificate GPA in Bangladesh from your subject marks. It converts each subject’s total into a letter grade and grade point, applies the fourth subject bonus, checks for failures and shows the final GPA on the 5.00 scale.

HSC results are reported as GPA, not as a university-style CGPA. If you are already at university, use the CGPA Calculator for your semester results, or the Percentage Converter when a form asks for a percentage.

Understand the Numbers

How HSC GPA Is Calculated

Add the grade points of your six main subjects, add the fourth subject bonus, then divide by six. The final GPA is capped at 5.00.

GPA = (main GP total + optional bonus) ÷ 6

Bangla’s two papers form one subject, and the same applies to other two-paper subjects. This calculator divides total marks by the selected maximum to find the percentage, then applies fixed grade bands. It never averages seven subjects and never rounds a percentage up to the next grade.

How the Fourth Subject Works

Only grade points above 2.00 contribute: bonus = max(0, optional GP − 2.00). An A+ adds 3.00, A adds 2.00, A− adds 1.50 and B adds 1.00. C, D and F add zero. These are extra points in the total, not a direct increase of the same size in your GPA.

Fixed Grade Bands

HSC Grading System

These marks apply to a 100-mark subject. For a 200-mark subject, use the equivalent percentage: A+ starts at 160.

For fractional percentages from combined totals, each band ends just below the next threshold: 79.5% is A, not A+.

Pass, Fail and GPA 5

Failing a Subject, GPA 5 and Golden A+

An F in any main subject means the overall result is Fail, even if the other grade points would give a high average. The calculator names the failed subjects and withholds the final numerical GPA.

An F in the optional subject adds no bonus. It does not cause an overall failure when all six main subjects pass. Do not use a high combined total to override a known component failure.

GPA 5.00 can be reached with the optional bonus even when some main subjects have an A. “Golden A+” is an informal term for A+ in every subject, including the optional subject. It is not a separate grade calculated here.
