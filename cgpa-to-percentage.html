Short Answer

How to Convert CGPA to Percentage

For a 10-point CGPA, multiply it by 9.5. CBSE publishes this multiplier as the indicative percentage for its grade-point results, so a CGPA of 8.2 becomes 8.2 × 9.5 = 77.9%.

To go the other way, divide the percentage by 9.5. A result of 85% is 85 ÷ 9.5 = 8.95 CGPA.

The 9.5 rule is for CGPA out of 10. A CGPA out of 4 works differently: divide by 4 and multiply by 100, so 3.5 becomes 87.5%. The sections below give the formula and a worked value for each scale.

4.33 and 7.0 Scales

The calculator has no 4.33 or 7.0 option. For these scales, divide your CGPA by the top score and multiply by 100. On a 4.33 scale, 3.68 ÷ 4.33 × 100 is about 85%. On a 7.0 scale, 5.95 ÷ 7 × 100 is 85%. This is a straight proportion and not an official policy, so confirm it with your university if the result goes on a form.

To get your CGPA on these scales from course grades, use the Canadian 4.33 GPA calculator or the Australian 7-point GPA calculator.

Marks

How to Find Marks From CGPA

A CGPA is built from grade points, so marks come from the percentage. Convert first, then apply the total marks.

1

Convert CGPA to Percentage

Use the formula for your scale. With the CBSE rule, multiply the CGPA by 9.5.

2

Apply the Total Marks

Marks = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Total Marks.

Out of 500 Marks
8.2 CGPA = 389.5
8.2 × 9.5 = 77.9%. Then 77.9 ÷ 100 × 500 = 389.5 marks.
Out of 1000 Marks
8.2 CGPA = 779
The same 77.9% gives 77.9 ÷ 100 × 1000 = 779 marks.

The figure is indicative. Your marksheet or university notice is the record that counts.

Choosing a Formula

Which CGPA to Percentage Formula to Use

No single formula fits every institution. These are the common cases.

CBSE Results
CGPA × 9.5
CBSE publishes 9.5 × CGPA as the indicative percentage for its grade-point results.
Technical Institutions
(CGPA − 0.75) × 10
Some institutions follow the AICTE formula. Use it when your institution's notification names it.
University Rules
Read the notification
Some universities state their own multiplier or issue a conversion certificate. That rule replaces any general formula.
Applications Abroad
Ask the receiving office
Some admission offices and credential evaluators state which conversion they accept. Read their instructions before you submit.

Bangladesh and Other 4.0 Scale Results

If your CGPA is out of 4.00, choose US 4.0 Scale (÷ 4.0 × 100). A CGPA of 3.75 becomes 93.75%. For an official percentage on a form, ask your university for a conversion statement. If you still need the CGPA itself, use the NSU, BRAC and IUB CGPA calculator or the Bangladesh CGPA calculator.

These are general patterns, not official policy. Last updated 25 October 2026.

Avoid These Errors

Common CGPA to Percentage Mistakes

⚠

Using 9.5 on a CGPA Out of 4

A 3.5 CGPA times 9.5 gives 33.25%, which is wrong. On a 4.0 scale the same CGPA is 87.5%.

⚠

Using a Semester GPA

A semester GPA covers one term. The CGPA covers all terms, so convert the cumulative figure. See CGPA vs GPA for the difference.

⚠

Rounding Before You Convert

8.37 × 9.5 is 79.52%, while 8.4 × 9.5 is 79.8%. Enter the CGPA with every decimal on your result.

⚠

Typing a Percentage in the CGPA Field

Entering 85 on the CGPA tab triggers a range error. Switch to the Percentage → CGPA tab for marks.

⚠

Treating the Result as Official

The calculator gives an approximate conversion. Your transcript or conversion certificate is the document that counts.
