Overview

IELTS Band Score Calculator for Bangladesh: What It Does

This IELTS calculator does two jobs. It combines your Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking bands into the overall band that universities and visa offices ask for, and it converts your raw Listening or Reading score (correct answers out of 40) into a band.

IDP and the British Council both run IELTS in Bangladesh, and both report results on the same 0 to 9 scale with the same rounding rule, so one calculator fits whichever you booked. It works for IELTS Academic and General Training, on paper or on computer.

Understand the Numbers

How the Overall IELTS Band Score Is Calculated

Your overall band is the average of the four section bands, rounded to the nearest half or whole band. Each section counts equally.

Overall = (Listening + Reading + Writing + Speaking) ÷ 4, rounded to the nearest 0.5

The rounding rule is specific to IELTS. An average ending in .25 rounds up to the next half band, and one ending in .75 rounds up to the next whole band. Rounding happens once, on the average, not on each section.

Because the average moves in steps of 0.125, one extra mark in a single section can lift the overall band by half a band. Check the Overall tab before a retake decision, not after.

Worked Example

IELTS Score Calculation Example

Suppose a candidate answers 30 Listening questions and 33 Academic Reading questions correctly, and receives 6.5 in Writing and 6.5 in Speaking.

Listening: 30 correct7.0
Academic Reading: 33 correct7.5
Writing + Speaking6.5 + 6.5
Average: (7.0 + 7.5 + 6.5 + 6.5) ÷ 4 = 6.875Overall 7.0

The average 6.875 is above 6.75, so it rounds up to a whole band. The same candidate with 6.0 in Speaking would average 6.75 and still reach overall 7.0, while 5.5 would give 6.625 and an overall 6.5. These are illustrations, not an official result.

A second case shows why section minimums matter. Take 7.0 in Listening, 6.5 in Reading, 6.0 in Writing and 6.5 in Speaking. The average is 6.5, so the overall band holds at 6.5. A target of overall 6.5 is met, but a rule of no band below 6.5 is not.

Overall Band Targets and Section Minimums

Many universities and visa programmes ask for an overall band plus a minimum in each section. A candidate can reach the overall target and still miss a section requirement, so check both. The overall band needs an average of at least:

Requirements are usually written in one of three ways:

How IELTS requirements are usually written
RequirementWhat it means for your four bands
Overall 6.5The average must round to 6.5. Sections can differ, for example 7.0, 6.5, 6.0, 6.5.
Overall 6.5, no band below 6.0The overall band must be 6.5 or higher and every section must be 6.0 or higher.
6.0 in each bandEvery section must be at least 6.0, so the overall band is at least 6.0 too.

Institutions and immigration authorities publish their own numbers and change them. Confirm the current requirement with your university, employer or immigration authority before you book.

Reading Your Result

What Each IELTS Band Means

IELTS gives every band a skill level name. Bands 1 to 3 describe very limited use of English, so most admission and visa requirements start at band 4 or higher.

In Bangladesh

Taking IELTS in Bangladesh: IDP, British Council and Your Result

In Bangladesh, IELTS is offered by IDP and by the British Council, on paper and on computer. Both providers publish their own test dates, centres and fees, so this page does not list them. Check the IDP Bangladesh test dates page or the British Council Bangladesh IELTS page for current options.

Your Test Report Form shows the four section bands and the overall band. Use this calculator to estimate before the result arrives, and treat the Test Report Form as the final record. UK study routes may require IELTS for UKVI, which is scored the same way as standard IELTS, so confirm the required test type with your university.
