Overview

What Is the IELTS Band Score Calculator?

The IELTS band score calculator does two jobs. It combines your Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking bands into the overall band that universities and immigration offices ask for, and it converts your raw Listening or Reading score (correct answers out of 40) into a band.

Use it while you practise with mock tests, to see how many more correct answers lift your band, or to check what overall band your four section scores add up to. If you are moving on to study, the CGPA Calculator and the CGPA for scholarships guide cover the academic side of an application.

Understand the Numbers

How the Overall IELTS Band Is Calculated

Your overall band is the average of the four section bands, rounded to the nearest half or whole band.

Overall = (Listening + Reading + Writing + Speaking) ÷ 4, rounded to the nearest 0.5

The rounding rule is specific to IELTS. An average ending in .25 rounds up to the next half band, and one ending in .75 rounds up to the next whole band.

Listening and Reading bands come from raw scores, while Writing and Speaking are marked directly against band descriptors. Only the four final bands enter the average.

Worked Example

IELTS Score Calculation Example

Suppose a candidate answers 30 Listening questions and 33 Academic Reading questions correctly, and receives 6.5 in Writing and 6.5 in Speaking.

Listening: 30 correct7.0
Academic Reading: 33 correct7.5
Writing + Speaking6.5 + 6.5
Average: (7.0 + 7.5 + 6.5 + 6.5) ÷ 4 = 6.875Overall 7.0

The average 6.875 is above 6.75, so it rounds up to a whole band. The same candidate with 6.0 in Speaking would average 6.75 and still reach overall 7.0, while 5.5 would give 6.625 and an overall 6.5. These are illustrations, not an official result.
