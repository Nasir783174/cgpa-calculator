Overview

What Is the SSC GPA Calculator?

The SSC GPA calculator works out your Secondary School Certificate GPA in Bangladesh from your subject grades or marks. It converts each subject's total into a letter grade and grade point, applies the fourth subject bonus, checks for failures and shows the final GPA on the 5.00 scale.

SSC results are reported as GPA, not as a university-style CGPA. After SSC, use the HSC Result Calculator for your college results, the CGPA Calculator once you reach university, or the Percentage Converter when a form asks for a percentage.

Step-by-Step

How to Use This SSC GPA Calculator

1

Select Your Group

Choose Science, Business Studies or Humanities. The subject list changes to match your group. Science counts 9 main subjects; Business Studies and Humanities count 10.

2

Choose Grade or Marks

Choose Grade to pick each subject's letter grade straight from your marksheet, or Marks to type each subject's total. Marks are out of 200 for Bangla and English (both papers together), 50 for ICT and 100 for most other subjects.

3

Set Your Fourth Subject

Pick your fourth subject and enter its grade or marks. Leave it as None if you do not have one. Only grade points above 2.00 count as bonus.

4

Use Grade Mode for Failed Parts

Marks mode cannot see separate paper, MCQ, written or practical pass marks. If your marksheet shows F in any part, switch to Grade and select F for that subject.

5

Press Calculate GPA

Your final GPA, pass or fail status, main subject GPA and fourth subject bonus appear in a result box. Open Show calculation to see every step.

Understand the Numbers

How SSC GPA Is Calculated

Add the grade points of your main subjects, add the fourth subject bonus, then divide by the number of main subjects. Science has 9 main subjects; Business Studies and Humanities have 10. The final GPA is capped at 5.00.

GPA = (main GP total + fourth subject bonus) ÷ number of main subjects

Bangla’s two papers form one subject, and so do English’s. This calculator divides total marks by the selected maximum to find the percentage, then applies fixed grade bands. It never rounds a percentage up to the next grade.

How the Fourth Subject Works

Only grade points above 2.00 contribute: bonus = max(0, fourth subject GP − 2.00). An A+ adds 3.00, A adds 2.00, A− adds 1.50 and B adds 1.00. C, D and F add zero. These are extra points in the total, not a direct increase of the same size in your GPA, and the fourth subject is never part of the divisor.

Fixed Grade Bands

SSC Grading System

These marks apply to a 100-mark subject. For a 200-mark subject, use the equivalent percentage: A+ starts at 160. For a 50-mark subject such as ICT, A+ starts at 40.

For fractional percentages from combined totals, each band ends just below the next threshold: 79.5% is A, not A+.

Worked Examples

SSC GPA Calculation Example

Suppose a Science student has A+ in six of the nine main subjects and A in the other three, and earns A+ in the fourth subject.

Main GP total: 6 × 5 + 3 × 442.00
Fourth subject bonus: 5 − 2+3.00
Effective total45.00
Final GPA: 45 ÷ 95.00

A Humanities student with 10 main subjects, a main grade point total of 41.00 and an A− in the fourth subject gets a bonus of 1.50. The GPA is (41.00 + 1.50) ÷ 10 = 4.25.

These are illustrations, not a student’s official result.

Pass, Fail and GPA 5

Failing a Subject, GPA 5 and Golden A+

An F in any main subject means the overall result is Fail, even if the other grade points would give a high average. The calculator names the failed subjects and withholds the final numerical GPA.

An F in the fourth subject adds no bonus. It does not cause an overall failure when all main subjects pass. Do not use a high combined total to override a known component failure.

GPA 5.00 can be reached with the fourth subject bonus even when some main subjects have an A. “Golden A+” is an informal term for A+ in every subject, including the fourth subject. It is not a separate grade calculated here.
