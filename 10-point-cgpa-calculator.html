Quick Start

How to Use This 10 Point CGPA Calculator

Calculate CGPA from courses

Under Semester 1, enter each course's credits and choose its grade. Select Add Semester for more terms. The Overall CGPA card updates as you type, and every semester shows its own SGPA.

Convert SGPA to CGPA

If you only have SGPAs from your grade cards, use the SGPA → CGPA table. Enter each semester's SGPA and its total credits.

Plan a target CGPA

The Target CGPA card picks up your current CGPA and completed credits from the course table. Enter the CGPA you want and the credits you still have left to see the average GPA you need. Download Report saves your semesters and target plan as an HTML file.

How your entries are handled

A course with credits but no grade is left out until you pick a grade. A course name entered twice is counted once, with the higher grade. Everything runs in your browser, and nothing you enter is sent to our servers.

Compatibility

Which Institutes Can Use This Calculator?

Any course-based program that reports results out of 10 with credits works here, including B.Tech, BE, BSc, BCA, MCA, M.Tech, MBA and MSc at IITs, NITs, IIITs, VTU, Anna University, VIT, SRM, Manipal, KIIT, LPU, Amity and similar institutes.

Letter grades are not standardised across India, so match by grade points, not by letter. IISc's 10 point scale gives A+ ten points, and NIT Delhi's published grading rules also list A+ as 10, while A+ in this calculator is 9. For such a grade, choose O (10.00) from the dropdown.

If your grade points are not in the dropdown, or you already have SGPAs, use the SGPA → CGPA table with the SGPA printed on your grade card. The CGPA on your official grade card always takes priority over an online result.

Step-by-Step

How to Calculate CGPA on a 10 Point Scale

Multiply each course's grade points by its credits, add up these credit points, then divide by the total credits.

CGPA =
Σ (Grade Point × Credits) Σ Credits

If every course has the same credits, CGPA is the plain average of grade points. For example, grade points 9, 8, 10, 7 and 8 give (9 + 8 + 10 + 7 + 8) ÷ 5 = 8.4.

Example with different credits

CourseCreditsGradeGrade pointsCredit points
Mathematics4A+936
Physics3A824
Programming4O1040
English2A816
Electronics3B+721
Total16137

CGPA = 137 ÷ 16 = 8.56. Mathematics and Programming move the result more than English because they carry more credits. SGPA is calculated the same way, using only the courses of one semester.

SGPA to CGPA

How to Convert SGPA to CGPA

Weight each semester's SGPA by that semester's credits: CGPA = Σ(SGPA × semester credits) ÷ Σ semester credits.

SemesterSGPACreditsSGPA × credits
Semester 17.518135
Semester 29.026234
Total44369

CGPA = 369 ÷ 44 = 8.39. A plain average of the two SGPAs would give 8.25, so credits matter when semesters carry different credit loads.

In the SGPA → CGPA table, enter credits for every row to get this weighted result. If every credit box is empty, the calculator takes a simple average of the SGPAs, which is correct only when all semesters have equal credits. If some rows have credits and others do not, the rows without credits are left out and a warning appears.

Target CGPA

How to Find the GPA You Need for a Target CGPA

Required GPA = (Target CGPA × (completed credits + remaining credits) − Current CGPA × completed credits) ÷ remaining credits.

With a current CGPA of 7.8 over 80 completed credits, a target of 8.2 and 40 credits left, the required GPA is (8.2 × 120 − 7.8 × 80) ÷ 40 = (984 − 624) ÷ 40 = 9.00.

This is the average GPA needed across all remaining credits, not in each course. The calculator shows Not Possible when the answer is above 10, and Already Achieved! when the target is secured whatever you score in the remaining credits.

A 10 point CGPA is a cumulative grade point average on a scale of 0 to 10. Each course earns grade points out of 10, and your CGPA is the credit-weighted average across all completed semesters. SGPA covers one semester, while CGPA covers all of them.
