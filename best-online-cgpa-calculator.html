5 Best Online CGPA Calculators in 2026: Free and Accurate

Published on: 14/07/2026

Calculating CGPA looks simple, but one small mistake can change the result. You need the correct grade points, course credits, semester credits, and grading scale. That matters when you check scholarship eligibility, apply for internships, submit job forms, or plan an academic improvement strategy with our CGPA improvement guide.

Bangladeshi universities do not all use the same grade-point mapping. The University of Dhaka uses A+ as 4.00, A as 3.75, and A− as 3.50. BRAC University uses A as 4.00, A− as 3.70, and B+ as 3.30. Both systems max out at 4.00, but they assign different points to several letter grades.

📖 An online CGPA calculator is only accurate when its grading scale matches your university's official policy. We compared the five tools below on grade-scale support, credit-based calculation, semester management, ease of use, target planning, and relevance for students in Bangladesh.

The Formula Every Accurate Calculator Should Use

A strong calculator applies the credit-weighted CGPA formula:

CGPA = Total of (Grade Point × Course Credit) ÷ Total Completed Credits

This gives courses with higher credit values more influence on the final result — a four-credit course should affect your CGPA more than a one-credit lab course. You should never take a simple average of semester GPAs when the semesters carry different total credits; instead, each semester GPA must be weighted by its semester credits. 2. Calculator.net GPA Calculator

Calculator.net offers one of the more flexible GPA calculators available. It accepts course names, credits, and grades, lets students add more courses, separate courses by semester, and include a previous GPA with completed credits.

The default letter-grade system is mainly designed for North American institutions — A+ as 4.30, A as 4.00, A− as 3.70, B+ as 3.30, B as 3.00. Bangladeshi students should not use these default values without checking their university's grading rules first, or compare them directly using the GPA ↔ CGPA converter.

The calculator becomes more useful through its settings: students can select letter grades, percentages, or point values, or enter specific values for a different grading system. It also has a GPA planning calculator — enter current GPA, target GPA, current credits, and future credits to see the minimum GPA needed in additional courses.

The interface contains several settings, so a new user may need time to pick the right grade format. Entering the wrong default grade points can produce a mathematically correct result that still does not match a student's actual transcript.

Best for: Students who need custom grade values, percentage inputs, prior GPA support, or an international GPA calculator.
Main limitation: Its default grading scale does not match the scale used by many Bangladeshi public universities.
Verdict: An excellent technical calculator when students enter the right grade-point values — less convenient for students who want a ready-made local scale.

3. Collegedunia CGPA Calculator

Collegedunia offers an SGPA-to-CGPA calculator plus educational guides on GPA, SGPA, CGPA, and percentage conversion, mainly targeting Indian students on a 10-point grading scale. It is useful when a student already knows the SGPA for each semester and wants to average several semesters quickly instead of re-entering every course.

There is an important accuracy issue to watch for: Collegedunia's explanation adds all semester SGPAs and divides by the number of semesters. That method only works when every semester carries the same total credits — it can produce a different result when semester credit totals differ. Consider this example:

SemesterGPACredits
Semester 13.5015
Semester 23.0021

A simple average gives (3.50 + 3.00) ÷ 2 = 3.25. A credit-weighted calculation gives (3.50 × 15 + 3.00 × 21) ÷ 36 = 3.21. The weighted result is more suitable whenever semester credit totals differ — see our full CGPA calculation guide for more worked examples.

Best for: Students using a 10-point system or converting several equal-credit semester GPAs into a CGPA.
Main limitation: The SGPA averaging method may not account for different credit totals across semesters, and its grade tables mainly follow Indian academic systems.
Verdict: Useful for learning and quick SGPA conversion, but Bangladeshi students should not copy its grading scale or percentage formula without checking their own university rules.

4. CollegeDekho CGPA Calculator

CollegeDekho provides a detailed guide to the CGPA grading system, explaining grade points, CGPA calculation, CGPA-to-percentage conversion, and the difference between GPA and CGPA. Its examples mainly follow the Indian 10-point grading system, and it works better as a learning resource than as a full multi-semester calculator.

It shows students how to add grade points and divide by the number of subjects — for example, five subjects with grade points 10, 9, 8, 9, and 9 total 45, and dividing by five gives a CGPA of 9.0. That method only works when all subjects have equal weight, and university courses often carry different credit values, so Bangladeshi students should use a credit-weighted formula instead.

CollegeDekho also uses the formula Percentage = CGPA × 9.5, which is linked to Indian and CBSE-style conversion and is not a universal formula for Bangladeshi universities. Use only the conversion method stated by your own university, employer, or scholarship provider — or check ours on the CGPA to Percentage Calculator.

Best for: Students who want a simple explanation of CGPA and the Indian 10-point grading system.
Main limitation: It is not a full Bangladesh-focused course and semester calculator, and some formulas assume equal course weight.
Verdict: Helpful for understanding the basic idea of CGPA, but not the best option for calculating an official credit-weighted CGPA for a Bangladeshi university.

5. EasyCalculation CGPA Calculator

EasyCalculation offers a basic online CGPA calculator for finding a cumulative grade point average across multiple subjects, focused on completing the calculation quickly rather than offering the detailed planning features of the top two tools. It suits students who already understand their grading scale and just need a quick result without creating an account or working through several settings.

Basic calculators like this one require more care from the user — confirm whether it accepts course credits, grade points, letter grades, or a plain average of subjects, and check whether its scale matches a 4.00, 4.33, 5.00, 7.00, or 10.00 system. EasyCalculation does not offer the same level of Bangladesh-focused guidance, semester management, grading-scale selection, target planning, or reporting as the higher-ranked tools.

Best for: Quick and basic CGPA calculations with a small amount of data.
Main limitation: Fewer advanced features and less guidance about university-specific grading rules.
Verdict: Convenient for a fast estimate — students managing several semesters or different course credits will likely prefer a more detailed calculator such as the CGPA Calculator by Mohammad Nasir.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which online CGPA calculator is the most accurate?
The most accurate calculator is the one that matches your university's official grade points and calculation policy. For most Bangladeshi students, the calculator by Mohammad Nasir is the strongest starting point because it includes the common Bangladesh 4.00 scale and five other grading scales. Calculator.net can also be accurate when you enter custom point values — always compare a calculator's grade table with your university handbook before accepting the result.

Are online CGPA calculators free?
Yes, all five calculators reviewed here can be used without paying for the main calculation. Students should still avoid entering sensitive information — a CGPA calculation only needs grades and credit hours, never a student ID, password, transcript number, or national identification detail.

Can I calculate CGPA for any university?
You can calculate CGPA for any university when the calculator lets you enter the exact grade points and course credits it uses; a fixed-scale calculator may not work everywhere. Check how your university handles retakes, failed courses, transferred credits, non-credit courses, withdrawals, incomplete grades, and grade replacement, since these can change your final CGPA even when the basic formula is correct.

Which calculator supports multiple grading scales?
The calculator created by Mohammad Nasir supports Bangladesh Standard 4.00, North American 4.00, Indian 10-point, Nigerian 5.00, Australian 7.00, and Canadian 4.33 scales. Calculator.net also supports different inputs through its settings, including letter grades, percentages, or custom point values.

Is an online CGPA calculator better than manual calculation?
An online calculator is faster and reduces common arithmetic mistakes, especially with many courses across several semesters. Manual calculation is still useful for checking the result — multiply each course grade point by its credit, add the weighted points, and divide by total credits. If the manual and online results do not match, review the grading scale, credit values, repeated courses, and excluded courses using our What Is CGPA guide.
