About This Tool

What is a CGPA Calculator?

A CGPA calculator works out your Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) from your course grades and credit hours. Add your semesters, choose a grade for each course, and it shows your CGPA and the SGPA of each semester.

This calculator uses the 4.00 grading scale approved by the University Grants Commission (UGC) of Bangladesh (A+ = 4.00, A = 3.75, A− = 3.50 and so on). National University and most public universities follow this scale. Many private universities use their own tables, so see Which CGPA Scale Should You Use? before you start.

SGPA covers one semester. CGPA covers every semester you have completed, and it is the figure shown on your transcript.

Step-by-Step Guide

How to Use This CGPA Calculator

Follow these steps to get your CGPA.

1

Check your grading scale

This calculator uses the UGC 4.00 scale (A+ = 4.00, A = 3.75, A− = 3.50 … F = 0.00). If your university uses a different table, see Which CGPA Scale Should You Use?

2

Add your semesters

The calculator starts with one semester. Click Add Semester for each further semester. Semesters are numbered automatically.

3

Enter your courses

In each semester, type the course name, the credits (for example 3 or 1.5) and choose the grade. Click + Add Course for more courses. The SGPA of the semester updates as you type.

4

Enter retakes

If you retook a course, add it again with exactly the same course name. The calculator counts only the best grade for that course. Retake rules differ between universities, so check yours.

5

Read your CGPA

Your CGPA, total credits and number of semesters appear in the Overall CGPA panel, with a label such as Good or Very Good. The label is only a guide, not an official class. A bar at the top of the page keeps your CGPA in view as you scroll.

6

Set a target CGPA

In the Target CGPA section, enter the CGPA you want and the credits you have left. The calculator shows the GPA you need in those credits.

7

Download the report

Click Download Report to save your semesters, courses, SGPAs and CGPA as an HTML file that you can open in a browser or print.

Already know your SGPAs? Use the SGPA → CGPA section above. Enter each semester's SGPA and credits to get a credit-weighted CGPA.

Calculation Method

CGPA Formula and Calculation Example

CGPA is the total weighted grade points from all your completed courses, divided by the total credit hours you have taken.

CGPA Formula

The formula is:

CGPA =
Total Weighted Grade Points Total Credit Hours

Grade points

Each letter grade has a grade point, taken from the UGC grading table above (A+ = 4.00, A = 3.75, A− = 3.50 and so on).

Why credit hours matter

CGPA is a weighted average, so each course counts in proportion to its credits. An A in a 4-credit course adds more grade points than an A in a 2-credit course.

CGPA Calculation Example

Take a student who has completed these four courses:

Why It Matters

Why CGPA Is Important

CGPA is the number most people ask for when they look at your academic record. It is used in a few common places.

Scholarships. Many scholarships and stipends set a minimum CGPA. See our CGPA for Scholarships guide.

Jobs. Some employers ask for a minimum CGPA in their job circulars. See our CGPA for Jobs guide.

Higher studies. Master's and PhD programs, in Bangladesh and abroad, usually ask for a minimum CGPA or its equivalent.

Honours. Some universities give honours or a Dean's List place based on CGPA.

Academic Tips

How to Improve Your CGPA

Every course counts in proportion to its credits, so a better grade in a high-credit course raises your CGPA more than the same improvement in a low-credit course. Use the Target CGPA section to see what GPA you need in your remaining credits, and check your university's retake policy before you retake a course.

CGPA vs GPA

GPA and CGPA are related, but they cover different periods.

ScopeSingle semester or termEntire academic program
Also Known AsSGPA (Semester GPA)Overall / Cumulative GPA
Calculation PeriodOne term at a timeAll completed semesters
Use CaseTracking semester performanceScholarships, jobs, grad school
Resets Each Semester?Yes: fresh each termNo: running average
Impact of Bad SemesterOnly affects that semesterLowers your overall CGPA
Impact of Good SemesterDoesn't fix past semestersGradually improves CGPA
