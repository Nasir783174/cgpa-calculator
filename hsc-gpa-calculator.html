Overview

HSC GPA Calculator for Bangladesh Students

This HSC GPA calculator works out your Higher Secondary Certificate GPA on the 5.00 scale used by the nine general education boards of Bangladesh. Choose your group, enter a grade or the marks for each subject, add your 4th subject, and this Bangladesh HSC result calculator shows your GPA, pass or fail status and every calculation step.

In short: add the grade points of your six main subjects, add the 4th subject points above 2.00, divide by 6 and cap the result at 5.00. HSC results are reported as a GPA, not as a university CGPA. If you are already at university, use the CGPA Calculator for semester results, or the Percentage Converter when a form asks for a percentage.

Step-by-Step

How to Use This HSC GPA Calculator

1

Select Your Group

Choose Science, Humanities or Business Studies. The subject list changes to match your group, and you can swap the group subjects and the 4th subject from the drop-downs.

2

Pick a Grade or Enter Marks

Choose Grade to select each subject’s letter grade, or Marks to type the combined total of both papers and the full marks shown on your marksheet (100 or 200). Each subject counts once.

3

Add Your 4th Subject

Pick your 4th subject and its grade or marks, or leave it empty if you have none. If your marksheet shows F in any paper, MCQ, theory or practical part, choose Grade mode and select F, because totals alone cannot show this.

4

Press Calculate GPA

Your final GPA, pass or fail status, main subject GPA and 4th subject bonus appear in the result box below the button. Open Show calculation to see every step.

Understand the Numbers

How HSC GPA Is Calculated

Add the grade points of your six main subjects, add the 4th subject bonus, then divide by six. The final GPA is capped at 5.00.

GPA = (main GP total + 4th subject bonus) ÷ 6

The two papers of Bangla form one subject, and the same applies to other two-paper subjects. This calculator divides your total marks by the full marks you select to get a percentage, then applies fixed grade bands. It never averages seven subjects and never rounds a percentage up to the next grade. The GPA is shown to two decimal places.

How the 4th Subject Works

Only grade points above 2.00 count: bonus = max(0, 4th subject GP − 2.00). These are extra points in the total, not a direct rise of the same size in your GPA, because the total is divided by 6.

Worked Examples

HSC GPA Calculation Examples

Example 1: GPA 5.00 with the 4th subject

Main subject grade points are 5, 4, 5, 4, 5 and 4, and the 4th subject earns 5.

Main GP total27.00
4th subject bonus: 5 − 2+3.00
Total30.00
Final GPA: 30 ÷ 65.00

To see this in the calculator, pick A+, A, A+, A, A+, A for the main subjects and A+ for the 4th subject.

Example 2: GPA with and without the 4th subject

Main subject grades are A+, A+, A, A, A− and B, and the 4th subject is A.

Main GP total: 5 + 5 + 4 + 4 + 3.5 + 324.50
GPA without 4th subject: 24.50 ÷ 64.08
4th subject bonus: 4 − 2+2.00
Final GPA: 26.50 ÷ 64.42

Both examples are illustrations, not a student’s official result.

Pass, Fail and GPA 5

Failing a Subject, GPA 5 and Golden A+

An F in any main subject means the overall result is Fail, even if the other grade points would give a high average. The calculator names the failed subjects and withholds the final GPA.

An F in the 4th subject adds no bonus. It does not cause an overall failure when all six main subjects pass. Do not use a high combined total to override a known component failure.

GPA 5.00 can be reached with the 4th subject bonus even when some main subjects have an A. “Golden A+” is an informal term for A+ in every subject, including the 4th subject. It is not a separate grade calculated here.

For Admission

Combined SSC and HSC GPA

Many admission circulars add your SSC GPA and HSC GPA to get a combined GPA out of 10.00. With SSC 4.80 and HSC 4.50, the combined GPA is 9.30.

Each university or admission test sets its own minimum, its own weighting and whether the 4th subject is counted, so read the circular of the institution you apply to. Work out your SSC GPA with the SSC GPA Calculator.

After Your Result

Check Your Calculation Against the Board Result

The education boards publish official HSC results on educationboardresults.gov.bd. Use this calculator to check the GPA on your marksheet or to estimate it before you open it.

If the two numbers differ, check the grade or marks you entered, the full marks (100 or 200) of each subject, and whether any paper, MCQ, theory or practical part shows F. The board result is the final one. If you believe your marksheet has an error, the boards accept rescrutiny applications (board challenge) after the result, and each board announces its own dates and rules.
