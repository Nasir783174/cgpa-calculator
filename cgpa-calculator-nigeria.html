Overview

What Is a Nigeria CGPA Calculator?

A Nigeria CGPA calculator works out your Cumulative Grade Point Average on the 5.0 scale used by Nigerian universities. You enter each course with its credit unit and grade, and the tool returns your semester GPA (SGPA) and your overall CGPA. It also shows the GPA you need in your remaining credit units to reach a target CGPA, and it converts a list of SGPAs into a CGPA.

The calculator is built for university results on the 5.0 scale. Polytechnic ND and HND results use a different 4.0 scale, so they need a different tool.

Steps

How to Use the Nigerian CGPA Calculator

Step 1. In the first semester box, type the course name or code, the credit unit and the grade for each course. Use the same spelling every time you enter a course.

Step 2. Click Add Semester for each new semester. Every semester box shows its own SGPA.

Step 3. Read your overall CGPA in the Overall CGPA panel. It updates each time you change a grade or credit unit.

Step 4. To plan ahead, open the Target CGPA Planner. Your current CGPA and completed credits fill in automatically. Add your target and the credit units you still have left.

Step 5. If you only have semester results, use the SGPA to CGPA section and enter each SGPA with its credit units. Click Download Report to save your results as a file you can open and print.

Definition

What Is CGPA in Nigeria?

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It is the credit-weighted average of your grade points across every semester you have completed. Your CGPA changes each time new results are added, and it decides your final class of degree.

Nigerian result sheets use a few short terms. A course's credit unit (CU) is its weight. Grade point (GP) is the number tied to your letter grade. Total Credit Units (TCU) and Total Quality Points (TQP) are the two totals behind every GPA and CGPA. A GPA covers one semester, while a CGPA covers all of them. For a longer explanation, read what CGPA means and the difference between CGPA and GPA.

Scale Overview

Nigerian 5.0 Grading System and Grade Points

Most Nigerian universities convert a course score into a letter grade, and each letter carries a grade point from 0 to 5. The usual boundaries are below.

C50 - 593.00

Some universities set a different lowest pass mark or score range for an E, so confirm the boundaries in your institution's grading regulations. Polytechnics and monotechnics generally use a 4.0 scale, which this calculator does not cover.

Calculation

Nigerian CGPA Formula

Start with the quality points for each course:

Quality Points (QP) = Grade Point (GP) × Credit Unit (CU)

Add the quality points of all courses to get TQP, and add the credit units to get TCU. Then divide:

GPA or CGPA = Total Quality Points (TQP) ÷ Total Credit Units (TCU)

For a semester GPA, use only that semester's courses. For CGPA, use the totals from all semesters together. A 3-unit course with an A gives 5 × 3 = 15 quality points, and a 3-unit course with a C gives 3 × 3 = 9. Courses with more credit units move your result further. See the full guide on how to calculate CGPA for other grading scales.

GPA = 55 ÷ 13 = 4.23

Two semesters (CGPA)

Semester 1 has 20 credit units and 80 quality points, so its GPA is 4.00. Semester 2 has 18 credit units and 63 quality points, so its GPA is 3.50.

CGPA = (80 + 63) ÷ (20 + 18) = 143 ÷ 38 = 3.76

The plain average of 4.00 and 3.50 is 3.75, which is slightly off because the semesters carry different credit loads. CGPA always weights each semester by its credit units. The same method works for any department or university.

Planning

Target CGPA: What Do You Need to Reach First Class?

The Target CGPA Planner uses this formula:

Required GPA = (Target × (Completed + Remaining) - Current × Completed) ÷ Remaining

Possible example. You have a CGPA of 4.30 over 80 completed credit units, and 40 credit units remain. To finish on 4.50, you need (4.50 × 120 - 4.30 × 80) ÷ 40 = 4.90 across those remaining units.

Not possible example. With a CGPA of 4.20 over 80 credit units and 40 left, the same target needs (540 - 336) ÷ 40 = 5.10. That is above the 5.00 maximum, so the planner shows Not Possible. A lower target would then be the realistic goal. For ways to lift your average, see how to improve your CGPA.

Carryovers

Carryover and Repeated Courses

An F gives 0 grade points, but the course's credit units still count in your total, so a failed course lowers your CGPA. Passing it later can raise your CGPA again.

To handle a repeat in this calculator, add the course again in a new row with exactly the same course name or code and enter the new grade. When names match, the overall CGPA counts only the best grade for that course, and the other attempt is dimmed. Semester SGPA is not changed by this.

Universities differ on repeats. Some replace the old grade with the new one, while others keep every attempt on the record. If your university keeps both, your official CGPA will be lower than the figure here.

The label shown beside your CGPA in the calculator is a performance label, not an official class: Excellent (4.50 and above), Very Good (3.50 to 4.49), Good (3.00 to 3.49), Average (2.00 to 2.99), Pass (1.00 to 1.99) and Fail (below 1.00). Use the table above to find your class, and confirm the exact bands with your university.

Accuracy

Why Your Portal CGPA May Differ From the Calculator

The calculator rounds to two decimal places. Some portals truncate instead, so a result like 4.4987 can show as 4.49 there and 4.50 here. Other causes are different score boundaries, a different rule for repeated courses, and a wrong credit unit or grade entered by mistake. Compare your total credit units and total quality points with the portal's totals, and the gap usually shows up in one of them. If you plan to apply abroad, use the GPA converter for an estimate, and remember that foreign universities publish their own Nigerian CGPA equivalents.

Graduate Jobs
Set by employer
